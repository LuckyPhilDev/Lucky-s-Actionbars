# Lucky's Actionbars

Adds hold-to-page to the stock main action bar, no bar replacement needed.

## Features

- **Hold to page**: pick a page for Ctrl, Alt and Shift. Holding the key shows that page on Action Bar 1, and releasing it returns to whatever it showed before, including stance, form and stealth pages.
- **Any spare page**: page Action Bar 1 to its second page, to the contents of Action Bars 3 to 8, or to a form page your class does not use.
- **Keybinds follow the page**: while a modifier is held, your Action Bar 1 keys press the paged buttons. Any modifier key you have already bound to something else is left alone.
- **Action Bars 9 to 12**: four extra bars that show pages 7 to 10, built from the stock buttons. Move them in Edit Mode, where they snap to nearby bars, with a separate position for each layout. Selecting any action bar in Edit Mode lets you show or hide them.
- **One place for bar visibility**: show or hide Action Bars 2 to 12. Bars 2 to 8 stay in sync with the stock Action Bars options.
- **Out-of-range icons**: the whole icon turns red while your target is out of range, on every action bar.
- **Hide button text**: remove keybind text or macro names from every action button.
- **Works in combat**: paging runs through the game's secure state system.
- **Stays out of the way**: vehicles, override bars, possession and pet battles are never paged.

## Installation

Install `Luckys_Actionbars` in `World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns`. Lucky's Utils is bundled.

## Usage

1. Type `/luckybars` and pick a page for each modifier. Ctrl pages to Action Bar 1's second page by default.
2. Hold the modifier and drag spells onto Action Bar 1 to fill its page.
3. Hold the modifier and press your Action Bar 1 keys to cast from it.

## Slash Commands

| Command | Action |
|---|---|
| `/luckybars` | Opens the settings |

## Settings

Under **Paging**, choose a page or Off for Ctrl, Alt and Shift. Under **Bars**, choose which of Action Bars 2 to 12 are shown. Shift-1 to Shift-6 switch action bar pages by default, so unbind them in Key Bindings if you page with Shift.
